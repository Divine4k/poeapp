                        Loot counting

                        Loot is counted by reading your inventory from the screen while it is open in game.

                        GGG's rules allow one screen read per key press or click of your own. Reading on your inventory key, or after your own click, stays within that. Reading by itself does not: it is off until you switch it on, and it is your own risk.

                        About

                        This app reads the game log, the clipboard and the screen. It only sends something to the game when you press a key or click a button: one Ctrl+Alt+C on a check key, if copying is switched on, and one chat line for each trade button or trade key. The steps under "Done for me after a trade" are the one exception, and only when you switch them on. It never touches game memory or files. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Grinding Gear Games. Prices come from poe.ninja.
